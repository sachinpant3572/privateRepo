Registered decks (a ramp: an oriented box with a slope profile; a strip: a polyline with width and a height profile, the shape bridges will use) bucketed in a grid. 2. Vehicle air state (vehicle.gd): ground from Ground.height with the car's own y as hint. Track airtime and emit a signal on landing with air time, distance and peak height. Small bumps and normal road crests must NOT launch cars (no jitter at highway speed).
