# Open-world crime game concept (Gangstar-style, 2D isometric)

Status: **concept**. Captured Oct 25, 2026. The first write-up of this idea was
lost when a cloud session restarted; this file is the re-sent version.

Working title needed: "Gangstar" is Gameloft's trademark and can't be used.

## The idea (as described)

An Android and iOS open-world crime game in the spirit of the Gangstar series
(Rio, New Orleans, Vegas) and GTA San Andreas, in a **2D isometric, cartoon-like
style** like the older Gangstar games, with a very lively world and a much
bigger map.

### World

- Multiple cities on one map, each with a distinct identity (like San Andreas),
  plus **one island** away from the main map.
- Cities, towns, counties, suburbs, parks, desert, mountains, oceans, lakes,
  military installations, airports, train and bus stations, bridges, secret
  places and hidden locations.
- Deep exploration: lots to find, shop, buy and loot, so players find something
  new every time.

### Getting around

- **Drive/ride**: cars, bikes, bicycles, trucks, planes, helicopters, boats,
  cruise ships.
- **Public transport**: buses, trams, trains, metro, airships, taxis, airports.

### Things to do

- **Story**: engaging, immersive storyline and world lore. Missions from small
  to large scale, with stakes rising as the player progresses.
- **Side missions and jobs**: well designed, each offering something new, with
  Soulslike-style discovery (Dark Souls, Elden Ring, Crimson Desert).
- **Factions**: fight alongside or against them.
- **Friends**: relationships to build.
- **Combat**: several weapons; new fighting styles to learn.
- **Sports**, each with its own progression: basketball, boxing, baseball,
  cricket, football, table tennis, badminton (indoor and outdoor).
- **Customization**: lots of clothing; vehicles to buy.
- Many other side activities.

### Sound design

- Engrossing, high-quality sound that matches the mood, themes, vibes and
  region of each place.
- Multiple soundtracks for different things, so players feel more immersed,
  engrossed and engaged.
- **Decision**: no licensed music. Soundtracks will be generated with AI.

### Quality bar

Top-notch storyline, exploration, build-up, model quality and sound design, in
the same 2D isometric cartoon look as the older Gangstar games.

## Question asked

How viable, engaging, immersive and retentive can it be on Android and iOS,
based on feedback and analytics for similar games so far?

See the assessment below.

## Assessment summary (Oct 25, 2026)

Figures are industry benchmarks recalled from public reports (GameAnalytics,
AppsFlyer, store pages) up to mid-2026, not freshly verified. Verify before
using them for business decisions.

**Verdict**: the genre is proven on mobile and the 2D isometric niche is
under-served at high quality, so the idea is viable. The scope as written is
not: it's a AAA-sized feature list. Viable as a phased project starting from
one city.

| Dimension | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Market demand | High | Gangstar Vegas (100M+ Google Play installs), GTA ports, and endless clones show sustained demand. |
| Engagement / immersion | High potential | Open-world sandbox plus story is among the stickiest mobile formats when the world feels alive. |
| Retention | Medium-high, only with live systems | Story-driven games drop off after the story; long-term retention needs repeatable loops (sports leagues, faction wars, businesses, events). |
| Differentiation | Medium-high | Premium-quality 2D isometric open world is rare; GTA Chinatown Wars proved the format plays well on touch. |
| Feasibility as written | Low | Multiple cities, an island, 15+ vehicle classes and 7 sports with progression is many years of a large team. |
| Feasibility, phased | Medium | One city, ground and water vehicles, 2 sports, a focused story arc. |

### Biggest risks

1. **Scope.** Every vehicle class and every sport is its own subsystem.
2. **Art pipeline.** True 2D sprites need 8 to 16 directions per vehicle and
   per animation, and clothing layers multiply that. Recommended: low-poly 3D
   with a fixed orthographic isometric camera (looks 2D, far cheaper to vary).
3. **Flight in isometric.** Altitude reads poorly in 2D; planes and helis need
   camera zoom-out and shadows, so treat them as a later phase.
4. **Monetization backlash.** Gangstar New Orleans' energy timers and heavy
   IAP were badly received; Vegas' lighter model did much better.
5. **Soulslike discovery on mobile.** Hidden content rewards dedicated players
   but short mobile sessions need clearer hooks; mix hidden and signposted.
6. **Content rating and stores.** Allowed on both stores (GTA is) with a 17+ /
   Mature rating; avoid real trademarks and brands.
7. **AI-generated music** (chosen over licensing). Watch for:
   - **Commercial rights**: use only a paid plan whose terms grant commercial
     use of the output; free tiers usually don't. Keep a record of the tool,
     plan and date for every track.
   - **Ownership**: purely AI-generated audio may not be protected by
     copyright (the US Copyright Office's current position), so others could
     reuse the tracks. Human editing, arranging and mixing strengthens the claim.
   - **Infringement**: never prompt with real artist or song names, and check
     tracks for close resemblance to existing songs.
   - **Quality and consistency**: curate hard and master everything to one
     standard, so regions sound distinct but the game sounds like one game.
   - **Size**: keep the download small with streaming or optional audio packs.

### Suggested phasing

1. **MVP**: one city (New Orleans-like), cars, bikes, boats, taxis, one story
   act, basketball and boxing, 2 factions, clothing shop.
2. **Expansion 1**: second city, trains and metro, more sports, faction wars.
3. **Expansion 2**: countryside, desert, military base, planes and helis.
4. **Expansion 3**: the island, airships, cruise ships.
