# Open-world crime game concept (Gangstar-style, classic top-down)

Status: **concept**. Captured Oct 25, 2026. The first prompt for this idea was
lost when a cloud session restarted; this file merges the re-sent version with
the recovered first prompt.

Working title needed: "Gangstar" is Gameloft's trademark and can't be used.
Proposed: **Solvera** (see `WORLD_BIBLE.md` for the world, names and lore).

## The idea (as described)

An Android and iOS open-world crime game in the spirit of the Gangstar series
(Rio, New Orleans, Vegas) and GTA San Andreas, in a **classic top-down, cartoon-like
style** like the older Gangstar games, with a very lively world and a much
bigger map.

### World

- Multiple cities on one map, each with a distinct identity (like San Andreas),
  plus **one island** away from the main map.
- Cities, towns, counties, suburbs, parks, desert, mountains, oceans, lakes,
  military installations, airports, train and bus stations, bridges, secret
  places and hidden locations.
- Deep exploration: lots to find, shop, buy and loot, so players find something
  new every time.

### Getting around

- **Drive/ride**: cars, bikes, bicycles, trucks, planes, helicopters, boats,
  cruise ships.
- **Public transport**: buses, trams, trains, metro, airships, taxis, airports.

### Things to do

- **Story**: engaging, immersive storyline and world lore. Missions from small
  to large scale, with stakes rising as the player progresses.
- **Side missions and jobs**: well designed, each offering something new, with
  Soulslike-style discovery (Dark Souls, Elden Ring, Crimson Desert).
- **Factions**: fight alongside or against them.
- **Friends**: relationships to build.
- **Combat**: several weapons; new fighting styles to learn.
- **Sports**, each with its own progression: basketball, boxing, baseball,
  cricket, football, table tennis, badminton (indoor and outdoor).
- **Customization**: lots of clothing; vehicles to buy.
- **Wanted system**: police response that escalates with the player's crimes.
- **Random events**: unscripted things happening around the world.
- **Street business**: run businesses on the street.
- **Property**: houses to buy.
- **Phone**: buy vehicles and other things through the in-game phone.
- **Shops**: enter shops and buy things inside.
- Many other side activities.

### Sound design

- Engrossing, high-quality sound that matches the mood, themes, vibes and
  region of each place.
- Multiple soundtracks for different things, so players feel more immersed,
  engrossed and engaged.
- **Decision**: no licensed music. Soundtracks will be generated with AI.

### Quality bar

Top-notch storyline, exploration, build-up, model quality and sound design, in
the classic top-down cartoon look of the Java-era Gangstar games but with much
better graphics.

Inspired by Gangstar, not a literal clone: mechanics and genre are free to
use; names, characters, maps, story and assets must be original.

## Suggested additions (from Claude)

Chosen for depth per unit of work on mobile; none are decided yet.

- **Phone as the hub**: contacts, mission offers, map, bank, vehicle and
  property marketplace, a social feed reacting to what the player did.
- **Territory control**: take blocks from rival factions; owned turf earns
  income and must be defended (GTA San Andreas gang wars).
- **Business empire**: street businesses grow into fronts with passive income
  and occasional attacks or supply runs to manage.
- **Safehouses**: owned houses act as save points, garage and wardrobe, and can
  be furnished.
- **Vehicle garage and tuning**: paint, parts, performance upgrades.
- **Skills that grow with use**: driving, shooting, stamina, strength, as in
  San Andreas.
- **Living world**: day/night and weather, NPC routines, traffic, police and
  gang patrols, per-neighbourhood reputation.
- **Random events with choices**: robberies, chases, accidents, people asking
  for help; choices move reputation.
- **Classic side jobs**: taxi, ambulance, firefighter, vigilante, courier,
  street racing, stunt jumps.
- **Heists**: multi-stage planned jobs as late-game set pieces.
- **Collectibles and secrets**: hidden packages, graffiti tags, landmarks to
  photograph, secret areas with lore.
- **Radio stations**: AI-generated music plus DJ chatter per city.
- **Photo mode**: shareable shots, which doubles as free marketing.
- **Branching story**: faction alignment changes missions and endings, which
  is the biggest lever for replayability; New Game+ after the ending.
- **Light online features**: leaderboards (sports, races), ghost races, weekly
  events, instead of real-time multiplayer at first.
- **Offline play, cloud save, controller support, low-end device mode.**
- **Avoid**: casino games built on simulated gambling. They raise age ratings
  and are restricted in some regions.

## Design pillar: high replayability and retention

**Requirement**: replayability and retention must be high and strong. Every
system is judged by whether it gives players a reason to come back or to play
again.

### Retention by time horizon

| Horizon | Goal | Systems |
|---|---|---|
| First session | Hook within minutes | Playable in under 30 s; opening mission with a chase and a quick win; first vehicle and safehouse within ~15 min; end on a cliffhanger or a pending reward. |
| Daily | A reason to open the game today | Daily jobs from phone contacts; business income to collect (capped, never punishing); random-event hotspots; a daily sports match; story in 5-10 minute chunks. |
| Weekly | Goals that span days | Faction turf wars; weekly challenges and events; sports and race leaderboards; rotating shop stock; new side-quest drops. |
| Monthly / seasonal | Something new to come back for | Themed seasons (carnival, Mardi Gras, holidays); free story episodes; new districts; a cosmetic season track. |
| Long term | Mastery and ownership | Completion %, collections, sports ranks, fighting styles, skills; growing property and business empire; repeatable heists and turf defence; New Game+. |

### Replayability

- **Branching story**: faction alignment and key choices change missions,
  allies and endings; several endings.
- **Multiple approaches** per mission: stealth, loud, vehicle-based.
- **Systemic world**: random events, NPC routines and wanted escalation create
  unscripted stories every session.
- **Variation in repeatable content**: side jobs and heists with different
  targets, routes and modifiers each time.
- **New Game+ and challenge modes**: harder police, hardcore mode, rampages,
  time trials.
- Optional, if budget allows: 2-3 starting backgrounds that change the opening
  city and first story act.

### What kills retention (never do)

- Energy or stamina timers that stop play (Gangstar New Orleans).
- Pay-to-win weapons or stats.
- Forced ads mid-mission; use only optional rewarded ads.
- Streaks that wipe progress when a day is missed.
- Paid loot boxes (restricted or banned in some countries).

Monetization should support retention: cosmetics, vehicles, story expansions,
optional rewarded ads, cosmetic season track.

### Measuring it

- Analytics from the first build: funnels, mission drop-off points, session
  length, where players quit.
- Soft launch in a few test markets to tune before the global release.
- Targets: D1 40%+, D7 20%+, D30 10%+ (top quartile for mobile).
- Live-ops needs a steady content pipeline; AI-assisted content (side-quest
  variants, radio, NPC lines) can help keep it affordable.

## Question asked

How viable, engaging, immersive and retentive can it be on Android and iOS,
based on feedback and analytics for similar games so far?

See the assessment below.

## Assessment summary (Oct 25, 2026)

Figures are industry benchmarks recalled from public reports (GameAnalytics,
AppsFlyer, store pages) up to mid-2026, not freshly verified. Verify before
using them for business decisions.

**Verdict**: the genre is proven on mobile and the top-down niche is
under-served at high quality, so the idea is viable. The scope as written is
not: it's a AAA-sized feature list. Viable as a phased project starting from
one city.

| Dimension | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Market demand | High | Gangstar Vegas (100M+ Google Play installs), GTA ports, and endless clones show sustained demand. |
| Engagement / immersion | High potential | Open-world sandbox plus story is among the stickiest mobile formats when the world feels alive. |
| Retention | High, by design (target D1 40% / D7 20% / D30 10%) | Story alone drops off after the ending; the design pillar adds daily, weekly and seasonal loops (turf wars, businesses, sports leagues, events). Needs ongoing live-ops content. |
| Replayability | High, by design | Branching story, multiple mission approaches, systemic world, New Game+ (see design pillar). Medium if the story ships linear. |
| Differentiation | Medium-high | Premium-quality top-down open world is rare; GTA Chinatown Wars proved the format plays well on touch. |
| Feasibility as written | Low | Multiple cities, an island, 15+ vehicle classes and 7 sports with progression is many years of a large team. |
| Feasibility, phased | Medium | One city, ground and water vehicles, 2 sports, a focused story arc. |

### Biggest risks

1. **Scope.** Every vehicle class and every sport is its own subsystem.
2. **Art pipeline.** True 2D sprites need 8 to 16 directions per vehicle and
   per animation, and clothing layers multiply that. Recommended: low-poly 3D
   with a fixed top-down camera (looks 2D, far cheaper to vary). **Decision**:
   classic top-down view only (see `MVP_DESIGN.md`, "Camera decision").
3. **Flight in top-down.** Altitude reads poorly in 2D; planes and helis need
   camera zoom-out and shadows, so treat them as a later phase.
4. **Monetization backlash.** Gangstar New Orleans' energy timers and heavy
   IAP were badly received; Vegas' lighter model did much better.
5. **Soulslike discovery on mobile.** Hidden content rewards dedicated players
   but short mobile sessions need clearer hooks; mix hidden and signposted.
6. **Content rating and stores.** Allowed on both stores (GTA is) with a 17+ /
   Mature rating; avoid real trademarks and brands.
7. **AI-generated music** (chosen over licensing). Watch for:
   - **Commercial rights**: use only a paid plan whose terms grant commercial
     use of the output; free tiers usually don't. Keep a record of the tool,
     plan and date for every track.
   - **Ownership**: purely AI-generated audio may not be protected by
     copyright (the US Copyright Office's current position), so others could
     reuse the tracks. Human editing, arranging and mixing strengthens the claim.
   - **Infringement**: never prompt with real artist or song names, and check
     tracks for close resemblance to existing songs.
   - **Quality and consistency**: curate hard and master everything to one
     standard, so regions sound distinct but the game sounds like one game.
   - **Size**: keep the download small with streaming or optional audio packs.
8. **Android specifics.** Huge range of devices, many low-end (especially in
   India); install size limits push big worlds into Play Asset Delivery;
   Android revenue is overwhelmingly free-to-play (ads and in-app purchases);
   offline play matters where connections are patchy.

### Suggested phasing

*Superseded by world v3 (`WORLD_V3_PLAN.md` sections 7 and 9): four cities on one state in
phases 1-6, then expansion cities from Porto Brisa on. Kept for the record.*

1. **MVP**: one city (New Orleans-like), cars, bikes, boats, taxis, one story
   act, basketball and boxing, 2 factions, clothing shop.
2. **Expansion 1**: second city, trains and metro, more sports, faction wars.
3. **Expansion 2**: countryside, desert, military base, planes and helis.
4. **Expansion 3**: the island, airships, cruise ships.
