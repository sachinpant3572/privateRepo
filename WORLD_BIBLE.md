# World bible: Solvera

Status: **draft**, Oct 26, 2026. Proposed names; nothing is final until
approved. Replaces the placeholders in `MVP_DESIGN.md` (Port Lafitte, Bayou
Wolves, Crescent Syndicate).

The map data lives in `game/data/world_map.json` (the game reads it); the
picture of it is `world_map.svg`.

## Working title: **SOLVERA**

Named after the state the game is set in: short, unique-sounding, works in
any language. Alternatives: *Ember & Tide*, *Gulf Kings*, *Three Crowns*.
Before committing, check store listings and trademarks for every name in this
document.

## The world in one paragraph

Solvera is a fictional Gulf-coast state where three cultures met and never
fully mixed: French river towns, Portuguese harbour cities and a Spanish-
American desert frontier. Its three cities feel like different countries. A
hurricane twelve years ago (the **Great Storm**) drowned half of Bellerive,
and the **Aurum Consortium** rebuilt it, buying flooded land for almost
nothing. Now Aurum is building the **Solvera Line**, a high-speed railway that
will tie the three cities together and put every neighbourhood along its route
up for sale. Every gang, cop and politician in the state wants a piece of the
line, or wants it stopped.

**Tone**: sunny, colourful and funny on the surface; real stakes underneath.
Cartoon violence, sharp satire of money and power, warm characters. No real
brands, no real people, no religious figures or institutions.

**Time**: the present day, so smartphones, social feeds and ride apps exist,
which supports the phone hub.

## History (what players can discover)

| When | What happened | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| ~300 years ago | French settlers found **Bellerive** on the crescent of the Morrow River | Lantern Quarter's old streets, lamp posts |
| ~200 years ago | Portuguese traders build **Porto Brisa** below the Pico Leão cliffs | Centro Velho, the Sentinel lighthouse |
| ~150 years ago | Silver rush in **Cinder Ridge**; the silver train vanishes in a tunnel collapse | Legend of the Silver Train (secret) |
| ~70 years ago | **Las Brasas** grows from a rail stop into a neon desert city | The Blaze, the Sign Yard |
| ~50 years ago | **Fort Granite** army base opens in Hollow Mesa; the government floods a valley to make **Lake Miravel** | Restricted base; drowned village (secret) |
| 12 years ago | **The Great Storm** hits Bellerive; the Floodline Wards are abandoned for years | Ruined streets, the drowned streetcar |
| 10 years ago | Aurum Consortium "rebuilds" Bellerive and moves into all three cities | Aurum Tower, Gilt District |
| Now | Construction of the Solvera Line begins | Building sites across the map; story spine |

## Places

### Bellerive (phase 1, inspired by New Orleans)

Jazz, balconies, humid nights, streetcars, the river always nearby. About
2 x 2 km on the crescent of the Morrow River.

| District | Feel | Controlled by | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lantern Quarter** | Gas lamps, iron balconies, jazz clubs, tourists | Neutral (tourist zone) | Story hub, shops, nightlife, Lantern Square |
| **Ironwharf** | Cranes, warehouses, container stacks on the river | Marrow Krewe | Smuggling, boats, heist targets, cruise terminal |
| **Gilt District** | Glass towers, Aurum Tower, Union Station | Aurum Consortium | High-security zone, the railway's start |
| **Magnolia Heights** | Mansions under oak trees | Aurum Consortium | Wealthy targets, property to buy |
| **Floodline Wards** | Colourful shotgun houses, storm scars, street courts | High Water crew (contested) | Turf wars, street business, basketball |
| **Blackwater Bayou** | Swamp, stilt houses, airboats | Marrow Krewe | Hidden places, lore, boat chases |

### Porto Brisa (phase 2, inspired by Rio de Janeiro)

Beaches, steep hillside neighbourhoods, carnival, football. On the east coast.

| District | Feel | Controlled by |
|---|---|---|
| **Praia Dourada** | Long beach, kiosks, volleyball, promenade | Os Corvos |
| **Morro Alto** | Hillside houses, stairways, rooftop views | Comando Maré |
| **Centro Velho** | Old colonial centre, metro hub, Avenida da Festa | Neutral |
| **Marina Azul** | Yachts, ferry to Isla Serena, seafood markets | Aurum Consortium |

### Las Brasas (phase 3, inspired by Las Vegas)

Neon, heat, desert nights, excess. In the north-west desert.

| District | Feel | Controlled by |
|---|---|---|
| **The Blaze** | Neon strip, shows, giant hotels, monorail | Velvet Aces |
| **Old Town Brasas** | Original rail town, pawn shops, diners | Neutral |
| **Ember Heights** | Gated suburbs, golf greens | Aurum Consortium |
| **Dust Yards** | Rail yards, scrap, bike bars | Dust Riders |

### Countryside (phase 3)

| Region | Feel | Key places |
|---|---|---|
| **Saltgrass Parish** | Bayou, fishing villages | Cypress Bend, Old Salt Mine |
| **Red Pine County** | Forests, farms, small towns | Dellwood, sawmill, county fair |
| **Lake Miravel** | Reservoir, cabins, water sports | Harlow, drowned village (secret) |
| **Cinder Ridge** | Mountains, mines, a winding pass | Cinder Peak Observatory, Silver Train tunnel (secret) |
| **Hollow Mesa** | Desert, mesas, rail stop | Sable Junction, Mesa Arch |
| **Fort Granite** | Army base, airfield, restricted | Jets, helicopters, the vault (late game) |
| **Golden Flats** | Coastal farmland, highways | Truck stops, crop dusting |
| **Verdant Hills** | Green hills between Bellerive and Porto Brisa | Dirt roads, villas |

### Isla Serena (phase 4)

A resort island off the south-east coast, and a free port where smugglers and
billionaires mix. Serena Town, the Serena Grand resort, an airstrip,
Smugglers' Cove, and the Sunken Casino offshore (secret dive).

## Getting around

- **Highways**: Route 1 (Gulf Highway, coast to coast), Route 40 (Desert
  Highway, Las Brasas to Bellerive), Route 7 (Ridge Road over the mountains).
- **Rail**: the **Solvera Line** (high-speed, being built during the story)
  and an old freight line from Ironwharf to Fort Granite.
- **City transport**: Bellerive streetcar, Porto Brisa metro, Las Brasas
  monorail, taxis everywhere.
- **Water**: ferry from Marina Azul to Isla Serena; cruise ship from
  Ironwharf; airboats in the bayou.
- **Air**: Bellerive International, Las Brasas Field, Isla Serena airstrip,
  Porto Brisa heliport; airship tours from Las Brasas over Cinder Ridge.

## Factions

| Faction | Who they are | Want | Home |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aurum Consortium** | Corporate empire with a philanthropic face and criminal muscle | Own the Solvera Line and everything along it | Gilt District, all cities |
| **Marrow Krewe** | Old bayou smuggling families; loyal, proud, old-school | Keep the river free and the bayou theirs | Blackwater Bayou, Ironwharf |
| **High Water** | Crew born from the storm-wrecked wards; part gang, part neighbourhood watch | Keep their homes from being sold off | Floodline Wards |
| **Comando Maré** | Disciplined hillside crew | Control Porto Brisa's port routes | Morro Alto |
| **Os Corvos** | Flashy beach-side rivals | Take Maré's routes and Aurum's money | Praia Dourada |
| **Velvet Aces** | Showbiz crime family running the strip's back rooms | Launder Aurum's money for a cut | The Blaze |
| **Dust Riders** | Desert biker gang | Run guns and parts along Route 40 | Dust Yards, Hollow Mesa |
| **Police** (Bellerive PD, Solvera State Police) | Some honest, many bought | Order, or a paycheck | Everywhere; not joinable |

## Key characters

- **Jules Moreau** (protagonist): grew up in the Floodline Wards, left after
  the Great Storm, became the best getaway driver in Porto Brisa. Comes home
  when their cousin goes missing. The name works for any gender, so the
  character can be fixed or customizable.
- **Teo Moreau**: Jules's cousin, a High Water member; disappears with a
  ledger of Aurum's bribes.
- **Mama Odette**: head of the Marrow Krewe; knew Jules's parents; trusts
  no one from the city.
- **Victor Hale**: Aurum's charming chairman; funds hospitals and parks,
  owns the people who run them.
- **Captain Dana Reyes**: Bellerive PD; honest, outnumbered, useful.
- **Lua Ferreira**: leader of Comando Maré; Jules's old boss in Porto Brisa.
- **Duke Calloway**: Velvet Aces boss; a showman who sells everyone out.

## Story spine (no mission detail yet)

1. **Act 1, Bellerive (street level)**: Jules returns, searches for Teo,
   finds the stolen ledger and is caught between the Marrow Krewe and Aurum.
   Ends with a faction choice.
2. **Act 2, Porto Brisa (the money route)**: the ledger leads to how Aurum
   moves its money through the port; Comando Maré and Os Corvos go to war.
3. **Act 3, Las Brasas and the desert (the line)**: the Solvera Line deals,
   Velvet Aces' laundering, Dust Riders' guns, a break-in at Fort Granite.
4. **Act 4, Isla Serena (the vault)**: Aurum's offshore vault and the finale.

**Endings**, set by faction choices along the way:
- **Crown**: Jules takes over Aurum's empire.
- **Ashes**: Jules burns it all down, the line included.
- **Light**: Jules exposes Aurum and the bought police, at a personal cost.

## Legends and secrets (for Soulslike-style discovery)

Each legend is hinted at in lore (radio, NPC lines, graffiti, old photos) and
pays off somewhere on the map.

- **The Drowned Streetcar** (Floodline Wards): a streetcar seen on flooded
  nights; following it leads to Teo's hideout.
- **The Silver Train** (Cinder Ridge): a train lost 150 years ago, sealed in
  a collapsed tunnel.
- **The Drowned Village** (Lake Miravel): rooftops visible in a drought year.
- **The Sunken Casino** (off Isla Serena): a hotel that slid into the sea.
- **The Sentinel's Light** (Porto Brisa): the lighthouse signals smugglers in
  code.
- **The Vault under Fort Granite**: what the army really stores there.
