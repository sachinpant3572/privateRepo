## Sourcing caveat

Every direct page fetch was blocked in this container (Wikipedia, the fan wikis,
rockstargames.com, the web archive). Every fact rests on search-result summaries
that quote and link those pages. Each note labels its facts as verified (two or
more sites agree), single source, conflict, inferred or memory. Treat single-source
numbers as rough.

## The numbers that shaped the map

| | San Andreas | GTA V | Solvera v3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Box | about 36 km² | about 76 km² (48 km² land) | 108 km² (12 x 9 km), about 75 km² land |
| Cities | 3 | 1 + towns | 4 + 16 towns, villages and ghost towns |
| City share of land | about 40 % | about 20-25 % | about 20 % |
| Links between neighbouring cities | 2-4, through named bridges or junctions | n/a | 2-3 per pair (highway, rail, often a back road) |
| Military | one big (Area 69), two small | one huge (Zancudo), 3-4 small sites | Fort Granite, plus 4 small sites |

GTA VI goes the V way (one big city, much more countryside). Solvera's brief, four
cities with country between them, follows San Andreas.

## Top lessons (ranked; details and sources in the notes)

1. **Theme cities with data:** one population, faction and police table per city and
   zone type, swapped by time of day. Cheapest life there is (SA popcycle; systems 6.1).
2. **Water and a few bridges are the skeleton:** they split the land, make chase
   chokepoints and story gates (SA 7.2).
3. **One town, one landmark, one faction per rural region**, and one sentence of mood
   for each (V 7.3, 7.7).
4. **Few rural roads, each with a character**, with dirt spurs to villages and sites
   (V 7.2). Keep roads wide for thumb steering (V 7.10).
5. **Travel by ticket and fade, not simulated rides:** scheduled flights, trains and
   ferries are a kiosk, a fade and arrival. GTA IV's ferries were cut over physics
   trouble (systems 6.2). The first trip between two cities should be driven.
6. **Gate the map with an in-world reason** (SA's earthquake, VC's hurricane); Solvera
   has the Great Storm and a dockers' strike (systems 6.3).
7. **One huge restricted base and several small sites** (SA 7.6, V 7.4).
8. **Fill long roads with events:** 10-30 short roadside events weighted by zone (V 7.8).
9. **Handling by archetype:** about 8-10 profiles cover planes, helicopters, airships and
   boats (systems 6.9).
10. **Avoid sketch-like cities:** one or two dense signature districts and a skyline per
    city, rather than thin grids everywhere (SA 7.10).
