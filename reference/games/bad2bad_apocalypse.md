### What Apocalypse changed from earlier entries

- **From Delta (side-scroller, lane defense) to Extinction and then Apocalypse (free-roam, top-down survival).** The big perspective change happened at Extinction. Apocalypse refined that approach. ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628104682831647193))
- **Random maps replaced by 60+ authored maps and regions**, gated by level, with repairable helipads for fast travel ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba&hl=en), [Fandom Mini-map](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Mini-map)).
- **Persistent base camp** with upgradeable facilities: bed, storage, crafting tool, shooting range, training center, cooking pit, self-propelled artillery and helicopter ([Fandom Base Camp](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Base_Camp), [Miraheze](https://bad2badtrilogy.miraheze.org/wiki/Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse_Game_Information)).
- **New survival layer**: day/night cycle, sleeping, fishing, cooking and gathering ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba&hl=en), [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/)).
- **Combined-arms support**: artillery, attack helicopters, combat drones and a pilotable "Battle Armor" mech ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2782610/Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse/)).
- **World Missions** on a global map, each "reconstructing" a nation ([Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/)).
- VGTimes reports the developer claims **three times more items and weapons** than the predecessors. It is unclear whether this compares with the earlier games or with the mobile version. ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/114623-bad-2-bad-apocalypse-a-post-apocalyptic-rpg-featuring-an-animal-squad-now-available-on-steam.html))

## 2. Camera and visual presentation

- **View**: a top-down / high-angle 2D view over hand-placed tile-and-prop maps. Characters are anthropomorphic animal soldiers. A TapTap reviewer calls the visuals "mostly flat 2D cartoon graphics" that fit the setting ([TapTap compilation](https://m.taptap.io/posts/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-reviews-1124452005)). **No text source states the exact camera angle or pitch.** My reading is that it is a near-overhead 3/4 view with sprite characters, based on general knowledge of store screenshots. This is **unverified** and should be checked against the store screenshots or the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCfGnuRrCoHQUy5k6pQzX2iw/about).
- **Scale**: the world is split into many discrete "maps" (zones) on a lettered and numbered grid, each tagged with a level (Lv 1 at the start, Lv 6–8 later) ([Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/)). The Fandom wiki describes "a large open-world map and smaller-scale maps" ([Game systems & Interface](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Game_systems_%26_Interface)). So it is a zone-streamed "open world" rather than one seamless map.
- **Art style**: cute or cartoon animals in realistic military gear. Hardcore Droid's reviewer pitches it as "a post-apocalyptic Zootopia focused on the military" ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/)). Gear shows on the sprite: helmets and night-vision goggles are visible equipment ([DAWINSTONE Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/DAWINSTONE/videos/412733996868893/)). Skins (firefighter, quarantine agent, season skins) are a cosmetic layer ([X](https://twitter.com/dawinstone/status/1625799846803427333), [App Store IAP](https://apps.apple.com/tc/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382)).
- **Night and lighting**: there is a night state. For PC, "night scenes improved" and the forest background was "completely renewed", with higher-resolution textures for characters, backgrounds and icons ([Steam PC features](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2782610/view/4453590197273100297)). So the mobile version shipped with lower-resolution textures as a performance or size trade-off.
- **Readability aids**:
    - Enemy health bars are **unlocked as a progression reward** by repairing the "EB" (Electronic Buddy) drone found in levels 1–3 ([Fandom guide](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/A_Guide_to_Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse), [EB](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/EB)).
    - A blue arrow points toward squad members you have not yet found ([Fandom guide](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/A_Guide_to_Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse)).
    - Battle Armor locations are marked in red ([gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-central-asia-map-gear-and-upgrades-locations.html)).
    - For PC, a "hidden monster display effect" signals nearby danger instead of relying on jump scares ([Game8](https://indie.game8.co/news/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-devs-posted-pc-version-features/)).
    - Item-quality indicators were added in v3.3.0 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382)).
- **HUD / UI**: left virtual joystick for movement and a fire button on the right. An **auto-aim ring** around the character locks onto enemies inside it, so the player can concentrate on movement ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/)). An auto-fire mode exists ([TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945)). Players have a mini-map and a full map that shows helipads ([Fandom Mini-map](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Mini-map)). Players asked for customizable button layouts, which suggests the layout is fixed ([search extract of TapTap reviews](https://www.taptap.io/app/244520/review)).
- **How the top-down view is used**: it makes squad formations readable (leader in front, followers behind or around; [Fandom](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Game_systems_%26_Interface)). It also supports kiting with the auto-aim ring and gives a clear view of incoming hordes, Wilder pits and drop pods.

## 3. Core gameplay loops

**Moment-to-moment (seconds)**: move the squad leader with the stick, let the auto-aim ring choose targets, kite enemies, and switch ammo or special abilities ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945)).

**Expedition loop (minutes)**:

1. Leave the base camp.
2. Fly by helicopter to a repaired helipad, or walk.
3. Explore a map of your level.
4. Loot buildings and crates, gather materials, fish, and kill Wilders and human factions.
5. Return to base before night, because night makes enemies stronger and more aggressive.
6. Sleep, cook, craft and upgrade.

Sources: [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/), [Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945). A player reports that one in-game day lasts about **20 real minutes** ([Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/), player claim).

**Meta loop (hours)**: find and recruit the scattered Delta Team members, upgrade the base camp (achievement for 28 upgrades; [Exophase](https://www.exophase.com/achievement/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-steam/3079811-upgrade-base-camp/)), move up gun generations and attachments, unlock higher-level maps, then take on World Missions to "reconstruct" nations ([Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/)).

**Late-game loop (criticized)**: after about 10 hours, a Steam reviewer describes the loop as "Go to ±your lvl area. Run around killing everything, looting everything, hoping you get the drop you're looking for. Repeat until you have 3/3" ([Steam review, Tamaster](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)).

## 4. World building and regions

- **Structure**: 60+ maps and regions ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba&hl=en)) laid out on a grid region map. Each zone is a separate map with a recommended level ([Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/)).
- **Story regions (fan wiki)**: Episode 1 Kazakhstan / Central Asia (Pan's plane is shot down by SAMs and he is stranded), Episode 2 Citadel (an ocean rig used as the Tailless Legion base), Episode 3 Olympus ([en.NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/B2B:%20%EC%95%84%ED%8F%AC%EC%B9%BC%EB%A6%BD%EC%8A%A4), [Fandom Citadel](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Citadel)). There are additional base camps, such as "Central Base Camp" and "Korea Base Camp" ([Fandom Goliath](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Amour:_Goliath)).
- **World Missions**: a separate global layer with missions in Spain, Italy, France, Myanmar, the Philippines and South Africa, under a "reconstruct nations" framing ([Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/), [Fandom Goliath](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Amour:_Goliath)).
- **Bases**: the base camp is a guaranteed safe zone where hostiles cannot enter ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/)). Its facilities are listed in §8.
- **Gating**:
    - Level-tagged zones are a soft gate: enemy strength rises sharply, and NamuWiki notes that Wilder health rises in higher-tier areas.
    - Repairing helipads costs 50 scrap per pad ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/)), and the Transport Helicopter must be repaired first ([Fandom Helicopter](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Helicopter)).
    - Story missions such as "Go to Apex" gate the next phase ([Fandom](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Game_systems_%26_Interface)).
- **Points of interest / density**: buildings to loot, fishing spots, Wilder Pits (dungeon-like nests), bunkers (VGTimes reports 50 bunkers added), Battle Armor sites, airdrops (disappear after one day or when you sleep), and wandering merchants on PC ([Fandom Map Locations](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Map_Locations), [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/114623-bad-2-bad-apocalypse-a-post-apocalyptic-rpg-featuring-an-animal-squad-now-available-on-steam.html), [Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/), [Game8](https://indie.game8.co/news/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-devs-posted-pc-version-features/)).
- **Secrets**: community "maps and item locations" guides exist for gear cores and upgrades, which shows hidden loot is a draw ([gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-central-asia-map-gear-and-upgrades-locations.html), [YEKPEAK](https://yekpeak.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-locations-guide/)). Dropped items never despawn ([Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/)).
- **Criticism**: "repetitive and generic" world design and "heavy asset recycling" ([Vaporlens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/2782610/bad_2_bad_apocalypse), [Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)). One reviewer needed 52 hours to clear about 70% of the map ([Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)).

## 5. Story and narrative progression

- **Premise**: in a world of anthropomorphic animals, the human "Tailless Legion" lost the war of Extinction. Chris Howard fired 11 AABMs that spread the "Wilder" virus worldwide, turning animals into zombies. Major Pan (Pan Coleman) and the Delta Team must survive, regroup and rebuild ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba&hl=en), [en.NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/B2B:%20%EC%95%84%ED%8F%AC%EC%B9%BC%EB%A6%BD%EC%8A%A4), [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628104682831647193)). Later factions include "Apex" and an alliance of the Tailless Legion with human extremists (fan account, same sources).
- **Delivery**: the opening is a plane crash that strands the hero alone. Recruiting the scattered squad is itself the first story arc ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/), [Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/)). Text and dialogue scenes are presumed. **No source describes cutscene format; unverified.** The story ends with a final fight against the TL main force and Duroc Moore, followed by the ending ([YouTube walkthrough title](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J-_nSP2j9P0)).
- **Characters**: a named, ranked roster with personalities and signature weapons, for example Maj. Pan Coleman (pistol, "Berserk": fire rate +15–35% as HP drops), Cong Woodman (sniper), Bagheera Hook (shotgun scout) and Rico Iglesias (DMR) ([Fandom List of Characters](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Characters)). New characters arrive through updates, for example CPT. Ratty Muller ([X](https://x.com/dawinstone/status/1668797390617481216)). Paid characters (7 in the PC DLC) include SSG James T. Suh, Lesser Pando, MSG Boomer Hogan and Cate Spears ([Steam DLC](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3110390/Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse__7_Character_Pack/)).
- **Tone**: cute animal characters with grim military-zombie apocalypse themes, played fairly straight. Hardcore Droid notes newcomers do not need the earlier games ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/)).
- **Continuity across the series** is a retention asset: three games share one cast and one escalating world conflict (Al-Qatala in Delta, then the Tailless Legion, then the Wilder virus) ([TapTap Extinction](https://www.taptap.io/app/158206)).

## 6. Missions and quests design

- **Main story missions** are linear and gate phases (for example "Go to Apex") ([Fandom](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Game_systems_%26_Interface)). One Steam critic calls the content "linear" ([Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)).
- **Recruitment quests** to find each squad member, guided by a blue arrow ([Fandom guide](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/A_Guide_to_Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse)).
- **World Missions** are country-by-country "reconstruction" operations that drop equipment cores. Their rewards were buffed in the Dec 2025 update ([Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/), [Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2782610/announcements)).
- **Wilder Pits and bunkers** are dungeon-style clears. One Steam thread criticizes a cross-marked bunker leading to a Wilder nest as "very long and tedious" with a weak reward (gloves and an AR-64) ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105220313853221/)).
- **Extermination missions** (2025) are kill-target missions added to address grind ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382), [Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2782610/announcements)).
- **Emergent threats**: a Hunter (enemy Battle Armor) arrives by drop ship if you stay out without sleeping for one day and one night on a map above level 3 and outside buildings ([Fandom Hunter](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Armour:_Hunter), [Steam achievement thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/7051042646089831245/)). The drop pod can land on your extraction helipad and block helicopter extraction ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105220313853221/)).
- VGTimes reports **20 added missions** in the PC release ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/114623-bad-2-bad-apocalypse-a-post-apocalyptic-rpg-featuring-an-animal-squad-now-available-on-steam.html)).

## 7. Combat and weapons

- **Guns**: 8 classes (AR, battle rifle, SMG, DMR, sniper, MG/LMG, shotgun, pistol), each with 26 variants across five "generations". Stats are Damage, Burst Speed, Control and Range; for example the MG-86 has 24 damage, 5.0 burst, 60 control and 425 range ([Fandom List of Weapons](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Weapons), [Miraheze Weapons](https://bad2badtrilogy.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons_(Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse))). The search extract was ambiguous about whether there are 26 variants in total or per class.
- **Attachments**: up to 7 slots. Grenade launchers fit only ARs and BRs ([Fandom](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Weapons)). A grenade launcher was also sold as an IAP ([App Store TC](https://apps.apple.com/tc/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382)).
- **Ammo**: free, infinite starter ammo that can sometimes deal no damage, plus crafted ammo (scrap, gunpowder, gasoline). Penetration and impact ammo types exist, and shock ammo helps against Wilder nests ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/), [Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105220313853221/)). This is a clear sink loop that keeps crafting relevant.
- **Weapon proficiency**: carries over when you upgrade a gun to a higher generation with the same name ([Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/)).
- **Melee**: **no source found; unverified.**
- **Squad combat**: you control only the leader, and up to 3 others follow and fight automatically, plus up to 2 mechanical companions (drones and similar) ([Fandom](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Game_systems_%26_Interface), [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3359520865)).
    - Formation slots 1 and 2 take the most damage ([Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/)).
    - Five formations trade stats; for example "Basic" raises leader defense and lowers follower damage ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/)).
    - Formation presets and leader skills were added in Dec 2025 ([Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2782610/announcements)).
    - Players rank DPS as LMG > SMG >>> BR ≥ AR >> Shotgun > DMR > SR ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4626979614631150577/)).
- **Fire support**: self-propelled artillery, attack helicopters, combat drones, and the pilotable Battle Armor "Goliath" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2782610/Bad_2_Bad_Apocalypse/), [Fandom SPA](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Self-propelled_Artillery)).
- **Enemies**:
    - Wilder zombies: Walker, Mos, Runner and Tanker per NamuWiki. Wilders get stronger at night and in higher-tier zones.
    - Human factions, chiefly the Tailless Legion ([en.NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/B2B:%20%EC%95%84%ED%8F%AC%EC%B9%BC%EB%A6%BD%EC%8A%A4), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945)).
- **Bosses**:
    - Wilder Queen (Lv 7): immobile, spawns eggs that hatch into stronger Wilders, fires slowing blobs.
    - Wilder King (Lv 8): slow, throws rocks, summons Wilders at every 25% HP lost.
    - Hikan Prez, Marcus Cage, and Dr Duroc Moore (Citadel) ([Fandom Bosses](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses), [Hostilities](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Hostilities), [Citadel](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Citadel)).
    - Three more bosses came in v3.3.0, and three new boss-level monsters were added for PC ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382), [Game8](https://indie.game8.co/news/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-devs-posted-pc-version-features/)).
- **Aiming on touch**: an auto-aim ring plus an optional auto-fire mode, so the thumbs only handle movement and abilities ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945)). The PC build switched to **manual targeting**, and at least one player asked for an auto-aim option back ([search extract, Steam "what changed" thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628104682831646872/)). This shows auto-aim was designed for touch input specifically.

## 8. Progression and upgrades

- **Character**: per-character stats and skills. Unless a skill is marked [Squad], it affects only its owner. **Mobility counts only for the current squad leader**, so players put their fastest unit in front ([Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3359520865)). Utility skills include Beo's material-collection skill (up to +20% material drops) ([Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3359520865)). Training center and shooting range facilities train characters ([Fandom Base Camp](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Base_Camp)).
- **Squad**: 4 organic units plus 2 mechanical units. You recruit them by finding them in the world ([Fandom](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Game_systems_%26_Interface)). Extra characters are sold (mobile IAP; PC DLC).
- **Gear**: gun generations 1–5, attachments, armor (prioritize it for slots 1 and 2), helmets and NVGs, item quality tiers, durability and repair costs (a reviewer says repair costs hit hard mid-to-late game) ([Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)). Equipment cores come from World Missions.
- **Crafting**: a crafting table at the north end of the base, ammo crafting, cooking, and "crafting and cooking mechanics" expanded on PC ([Steam beginner guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105046667572198/), [Game8](https://indie.game8.co/news/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-devs-posted-pc-version-features/)).
- **Base building**: these are upgrades to fixed facilities, not free placement ([Fandom Base Camp](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Base_Camp)).
    - Storage runs from Lv 1 to Lv 6: 20 / 35 / 62 / 95 / 140 / 195 slots. Skin storage holds up to 50 slots (raised from 30). "Extended Storage" from Lv 3 is a paid IAP.
    - The bed's recovery effect scales with its level.
    - The cooking pit upgrades with tools, duct tape, pipe, scrap iron and fuel.
    - Level-6 upgrades for storage, bed and range arrived in v3.1.1.
    - 28 total base upgrades unlock an achievement ([Exophase](https://www.exophase.com/achievement/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-steam/3079811-upgrade-base-camp/)).
- **Economy**: scrap is the universal currency for repairs (helipads cost 50 scrap) and is joined by fabric, duct tape, pipes, fuel, gunpowder and other materials ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/)). TL Circuit Boards are a late currency exchanged at a merchant (v3.3.0). Players call the economy "unbalanced" with "resource scarcity" ([Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2782610/bad_2_bad_apocalypse)). Storage space is tight early on ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4626979420667670357/)).
- **Pacing and gating**: zone levels, scrap costs and drop RNG set the pace. The PC rebalance adjusted "combat, drop rates, items" ([Steam PC features](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2782610/view/4453590197273100297)). A "farm mode" was added later to reduce the farming burden ([TapTap reviews](https://www.taptap.io/app/244520/review)). Monster HP was lowered in Dec 2025 ([Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2782610/announcements)). Repeated balance patches show the original F2P grind tuning was too heavy.

## 9. Vehicles, mounts, movement and physics

- **No drivable cars, trucks or bikes were found in any source.** On-foot squad movement is the core. Unverified, but no source mentions them.
- **Helicopter**: the Transport Helicopter is repaired at base and fast-travels to any repaired helipad. It is effectively a menu-based travel vehicle, not something you pilot ([Fandom Helicopter](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Helicopter)). The "Emergent Transportation" IAP (US$4.99) is probably an emergency extraction or travel item (exact function unverified) ([App Store search extract](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382)). Three are at Central Base Camp, two at Korea Base Camp and one in northern Spain (World Mission) ([Fandom Goliath](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Amour:_Goliath)). It is a power-fantasy set piece rather than a traversal vehicle. Its handling is **unverified**.

One formation gives up combat stats for squad movement speed ([Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/)). - **Physics**: no source describes physics simulation. Unverified; it looks like simple collision-based 2D movement.
- **Relevance to Solvera**: this is the main way B2B differs from a Gangstar-style game. Vehicles here are abstracted (fast travel, a support call-in, a mech set piece). Solvera will need a real driving model; B2B offers no template for that.

## 11. Player hooks and retention

- **Find your scattered squad**: a strong early goal with a clear reward, a new character in your party ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/)).
- **Day/night pressure plus a safe base**: about 20-minute days give natural session lengths; "get home before dark" is a built-in session end ([TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945)).
- **Gear chase**: gun generations, 7 attachment slots, 3/3 item sets, quality tiers and proficiency transfer.
- **Base visibly improving** across 28 upgrades.
- **Collection**: characters, skins, achievements.
- **Long-running updates**: new characters, bosses and missions through late 2025 (§1) give a reason to return. The developer was described as communicative with players ([NamuWiki](https://namu.wiki/w/B2B:%20%EC%95%84%ED%8F%AC%EC%B9%BC%EB%A6%BD%EC%8A%A4)) and runs a Naver cafe, Discord, X and Facebook ([X](https://x.com/dawinstone/status/1668797390617481216), [Discord](https://discord.com/game/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-1356553723316404331)).
- **Series loyalty**: the third game in a shared universe, with a fourth (Tombstone) in development ([X](https://x.com/dawinstone/status/1970399893362745506)).
- **Offline play**: praised in Play Store reviews ([search extract of Google Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba)).
- **Timed threats**: airdrops expire, and the Hunter appears if you never sleep. These punish over-long runs and push players into the base loop.

- **No energy system or gacha was found in any source** (unverified). Monetization is direct-purchase and materials-based.
- **Ads**: mostly optional rewarded ads ("ads are optional and give you items"; "no ad interruptions, popups, offers getting in the way") ([App Store review extract](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4665945)). Hardcore Droid calls it "very free-to-play friendly" ([Hardcore Droid](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/bad-2-bad-apocalypse-review/)).

**Complaints and mistakes**:

1. **Grind tuned for F2P** (material packs and paid storage sell relief from scarcity). This was the top complaint on all platforms, along with "slow progression" and "resource scarcity" ([Google Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba), [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2782610/bad_2_bad_apocalypse)). The developer then spent years patching it: farm mode, PC drop-rate rebalance, monster HP cuts and better World Mission rewards.
2. **The F2P economy carried over to the premium PC version.** A reviewer says MTX was removed but the grind and repair costs remain, and describes it as a "relaunch of a F2P, pay-to-win mobile title" ([Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)). Removing purchases without rebuilding the economy left the grind in place.
3. **Ad SDK instability**: players report crashes when ads load while online ([search extract of Google Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba)).
4. **Save loss** reports ("fails to save progress") ([same](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dawinstone.b2ba)). Specific causes are unverified.
5. **Payment bugs**: the Aug 2023 patch notes say "Fixed payment system" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-2-bad-apocalypse/id1661108382)).
6. **Selling storage space** (Extended Storage) when storage pressure is already a known pain point ([Fandom Base Camp](https://b2b-apocalypse.fandom.com/wiki/Base_Camp), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4626979420667670357/)).
7. **Long, low-reward dungeons** and **unfair emergent threats** (a drop pod blocking extraction) ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4628105220313853221/)).
8. **Asset recycling and content bloat**: about 52 hours of mostly repeated content ([Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)).
9. **Possible revenue cost**: the generous F2P design (optional ads, no energy, no gacha) earns goodwill and a 4.3–4.5★ rating but probably keeps ARPU low. Revenue data is not public, so this is **inference, unverified**. The PC move to a premium price (US$19.99 with no MTX) points to a strategy of monetizing the engaged core once instead of relying on whales.

## 13. Assets and production

- **Team**: a small indie team; size unpublished (§1).
- **Pipeline**: Unity with 2D sprite characters and tile/prop-based maps ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/2782610/ufs/)). The art direction is shared across the trilogy (same cast and art style), which lowers per-game art cost ([TapTap Extinction](https://www.taptap.io/app/158206)).
- **Modular character art**: helmets, NVGs and skins are separate layers ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/DAWINSTONE/videos/412733996868893/)). This lets one body rig carry many cosmetics, which are sold as IAP.
- **Asset reuse**: heavy, and players notice it ("immense content bloat due to heavy asset recycling") ([Steam review](https://steamcommunity.com/id/tho545454/recommended/2782610)). The 60+ maps are built from shared tiles and props.
- **Content volume**: 60+ maps; 8 weapon classes with 5 generations; 4 Battle Armour types; 10+ bosses; 7+ paid characters plus the base roster; 50 bunkers and 20 missions added for PC (VGTimes figures). Sources: §1, §7.
- **Handmade vs procedural**: Extinction used **randomly generated maps**; Apocalypse uses authored maps with fixed points of interest. This is a deliberate switch toward handmade content ([TapTap Extinction](https://www.taptap.io/app/158206), [Steam maps guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2782610/discussions/0/4693405423940184762/)).
- **PC remaster pass**: higher-resolution textures, "sound quality enhanced", a new forest background and better night scenes ([Steam PC features](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2782610/view/4453590197273100297)). This implies the mobile build used compressed or low-resolution assets and audio.
- **Audio**: no specific information found; unverified.

## 16. Lessons for Solvera

### Adopt
1. **Auto-aim ring plus an optional auto-fire toggle on touch**, with manual aim on gamepad, mouse or desktop. B2B's own PC switch shows this split works. On mobile, the thumbs should handle movement and abilities while the game handles target choice. Show the ring radius on screen so the rule is readable.
2. **Hand-authored zones with a recommended level shown on the map**, instead of procedural maps (B2B moved away from Extinction's random maps). For Solvera, tag each district with a level or heat value on the full map.
3. **A safe home base the player upgrades in visible tiers** (bed, storage, workshop and so on) as the session anchor and long-term goal. A Solvera hideout can do the same job: garage, armory, safehouse bed.
4. **Fast travel unlocked by repairing or claiming nodes** (B2B's helipads cost 50 scrap). This turns map discovery into a currency sink and makes travel a reward. In Solvera, claimable taxi stands or garages could fill this role.
5. **Early "recruit your crew" arc** with a guidance arrow. It is a strong first-hours goal and fits a crime game's gang-building fantasy.
6. **Progression-unlocked readability**, used sparingly. B2B unlocks enemy health bars via the EB drone. Solvera could unlock a "police scanner" or minimap threat icons in the same way, but should keep basic readability (danger indicators) on from the start, as B2B's PC version added.
7. **Modular sprite or cosmetic layers** (helmets, NVGs, skins on one rig) to multiply visible variety cheaply and support cosmetic IAP.
8. **Optional rewarded ads only, plus a one-time "Remove Ads" IAP** (US$7.99 in B2B). This protects the rating, and B2B kept 4.3–4.5★.

### Adapt
1. **Day/night with a "get home" incentive**: B2B uses about 20-minute days and stronger enemies at night. For Solvera, make night change gameplay (more police, different gangs, better heist payouts) rather than just raising difficulty, and do not force a return to base.
2. **Emergent hunter threat** (the B2B Hunter drop ship): use it as a wanted-level escalation, but never let it block extraction or cause unavoidable squad wipes. Telegraph it with a timer and warnings.
3. **Squad follower AI with leader-only mobility**: in Solvera, give crew followers formation presets (B2B added these in 2025) and keep the squad readable in 3/4 view. Cap the squad at 3–4 for clarity on phone screens.
4. **Ammo tiers as a crafting sink**: B2B's free unreliable ammo versus crafted special ammo is a good sink. Make the free tier reliable but weak; "sometimes deals no damage" reads as a bug.
5. **Gun generations with proficiency transfer** let players upgrade without losing investment. Keep the generation count small (B2B's 8 × 26 variants is bloat on a phone UI).
6. **Mech set piece → Solvera's set-piece vehicles** (tank, helicopter gunship), placed at fixed story moments. But Solvera must ship a real driving model, which B2B never had.
7. **Fixed facility upgrades rather than free-placement building**: far cheaper to build and test, and fits a top-down mobile game.

### Avoid
1. **Tuning the economy for F2P scarcity** (material packs, paid storage) and then charging a premium price elsewhere. B2B spent two years patching grind, drop rates and monster HP. Decide the business model first and tune drops for it.
2. **Selling relief from a pain point you created**: paid storage expansion while storage is the main early complaint.
3. **Long dungeons with weak rewards**: every optional clear should end in a guaranteed reward that matters.
4. **Ad SDK crashes and save loss**: load ads off the main thread, never mid-combat, and add autosave plus cloud save (Google Play Games / iCloud) from day one. Test with ads enabled online.
5. **Visible asset recycling across many zones**: fewer, denser districts beat 60 thinly varied maps. Solvera should prefer a handful of districts with distinct landmarks, palettes and props.
6. **Fixed, non-customizable touch layout**: ship button repositioning and scaling at launch.
7. **Shipping low-resolution textures and audio on mobile and fixing them only for PC**: build at target resolution and use Godot's import or compression settings per platform rather than separate asset sets.
8. **Linear story in an open world**: B2B is criticized as "linear". Let Solvera's crime missions branch by district or faction so the open world matters to the story.
